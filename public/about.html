01 ABOUT THE PICTURE

SUNDAY NIGHT SUICIDE REQUIEM

SNS Requiem is a fan-made reimagining of Friday Night Funkin': Sunday Night Suicide.

The project takes inspiration from the original experience while rebuilding its presentation, gameplay, music, characters, and atmosphere.

Requiem is designed as its own interpretation rather than a replacement for the original work.

02 THE IDEA

A LOST CARTOON RETURNS

Imagine discovering an old cartoon reel that should have never survived.

The picture flickers. The film scratches. The characters move.

And somewhere between the frames, something went wrong.
